## Description

Keep, cut, or pause the tools you pay for.

RiteStack gives your AI and developer subscriptions one place to live. Add your tools, monthly amounts, and renewal dates, then review what is coming next.

• See renewals and pause reminders in the next 14 days.
• Record Keep, Cut, or Pause decisions.
• Search and edit your complete inventory.
• Keep a receipt of the tools you marked cut.
• Save provider billing links so you can manage subscriptions yourself.
• Enable private, on-device renewal and pause reminders.
• Sync with your existing RiteStack account.

You enter your stack. RiteStack does not scan your inbox, connect to your bank, or cancel subscriptions for you. Recorded cuts are your decisions, not verified savings. Amounts are entered in USD.

Try the labeled sample stack without signing in. Inventory and cut receipts remain available free. Ritual actions use the access already associated with your RiteStack account. No purchases are offered inside this companion app.

## Review notes

RiteStack is a free standalone companion to the RiteStack web service. Under guideline 3.1.3(f), the iOS app contains no purchases or calls to action directing users to outside purchases. Existing account access is checked against the service. Review acceptance remains Apple's decision.

Tap “Explore a sample stack” on the sign-in screen to review all main screens and local decision/edit flows without credentials. Sample preview is visibly labeled and never sends changes to the backend. Exit via Account > Exit sample stack.

For a real account, enter an email address you can access and then the one-time code from the sign-in email. No password or social login is required. A newly created account receives the same service trial as the website. If Apple requests a persistent review identity, supply a dedicated review account through App Store Connect securely; never put its tokens or mailbox password in the repo.

To delete a real account: Account > Delete account > type DELETE > Permanently delete account. This removes the account and its inventory/profile. Other providers' subscriptions must still be managed with those providers.

Notifications are optional local notifications, not remote push. The app requests no photos, camera, contacts, location or tracking permission.

## Privacy answers to enter in App Store Connect

No tracking.
