## Full description

Keep, cut, or pause the tools you pay for.

RiteStack brings your AI and developer subscriptions into one place. Enter your tools, monthly USD amounts and renewal dates, then review what is coming next.

• Review upcoming renewals and pause dates.
• Record Keep, Cut and Pause decisions.
• Search, add and edit your inventory.
• Keep a record of tools you marked cut.
• Save provider billing links to manage subscriptions yourself.
• Use optional local reminders.
• Sync with your RiteStack account.

Explore a clearly labeled sample stack before signing in.

You enter your stack. RiteStack does not scan your inbox, connect to your bank, or cancel subscriptions for you. Cuts record your decisions, not verified savings. Amounts are entered in USD.

Inventory and cut receipts remain available free. Ritual actions use the access associated with your RiteStack account. No purchases are offered inside this companion app.

## Reviewer instructions draft

Use Explore sample stack to inspect local UI without an account. The sample is labeled and does not upload its data. Exit through Account.

For authenticated features, use the dedicated review access supplied privately in Play Console. Do not substitute a customer identity, a one-time code that will expire, or a promise that support will send a code later. Review access provisioning is a release checklist item.

Account deletion is under Account → Delete account. It deletes the signed-in account and its inventory across devices; use only the disposable review identity provided for this purpose. Other providers' subscriptions are not cancelled by this action.
